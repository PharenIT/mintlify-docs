---

Pharen Hub self-host v1 supports Linux `amd64`, Docker Engine, and Docker Compose 2.24 or newer. It is intended for startup and SME teams with a technical operator who can maintain DNS, TLS, backups, upgrades, and incident response.

You need a dedicated host, outbound HTTPS to `ghcr.io` and `billing.pharen.app`, sufficient storage for images/database/media/backups, and a DNS name with TCP 80/443 for production. Business production self-hosting requires a paid license for each installation. A Pharen-issued evaluation lasts 14 days and is not for production.

Use encrypted backup storage and keep an encrypted off-site copy. Do not reuse a developer `.env`, database, volume, customer credential, or production customer data for testing.
